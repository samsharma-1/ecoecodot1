# EcoTrack AI – MVP Enhancement Roadmap

## Current Status
Current MVP Score: **91.7**
Goal Score: **95+**

## Selected Core Implementations

### 1. AI Carbon Action Engine
- Personalized recommendations (based on lifestyle and habits)
- Estimated CO₂ savings (dynamic calculation)
- Difficulty estimation (low, medium, high effort)
- Weekly sustainability goals (adaptive and achievable)
- **[NEW] Action Impact ROI (Time/Cost investment vs. CO₂ saved)**

### 2. Carbon Savings Simulator
- Transport simulation (EV vs Gas, Public Transit vs Driving)
- Electricity simulation (Solar, appliance upgrades)
- Waste reduction simulation (Composting, recycling impact)
- Before vs After comparison
- **[NEW] Localized Context (adjusts simulation based on regional energy grid mix)**

### 3. Smart Carbon Forecasting
- Monthly forecasts
- Trend analysis (identifying seasonal spikes)
- Annual savings prediction
- Emission increase alerts
- **[NEW] Peer Benchmarking (compare forecasts with similar demographic profiles)**

### 4. EcoScore System
Levels:
- Beginner
- Eco Aware
- Green Advocate
- Climate Champion
- Earth Guardian
- **[NEW] Milestone Badges & Streaks (e.g., "30 Days Low-Carbon Transport")**

### 5. Real Eco Feed
- Climate news (aggregated from verified sources)
- Renewable energy updates
- Sustainability initiatives
- Personalized content
- **[NEW] Fact-checking Layer (AI validates news to prevent greenwashing)**

## Technical Architecture & Implementation Phases [NEW]

### Phase 1: Foundation & Data Integration
- Establish core carbon calculation algorithms based on EPA/IPCC standards.
- Integrate initial APIs for energy grid data and transport emissions.
- Launch user onboarding and baseline footprint assessment.

### Phase 2: AI & Simulation Rollout
- Deploy the AI Carbon Action Engine using a recommendation system (e.g., Collaborative Filtering + Content-based).
- Build the Carbon Savings Simulator with real-time interactive UI.
- Implement the EcoScore progression system and database tracking.

### Phase 3: Forecasting & EcoTwin Development
- Introduce time-series forecasting (e.g., ARIMA or Prophet) for Smart Carbon Forecasting.
- Develop the "EcoTwin" feature utilizing generative AI and predictive modeling to visualize future impact.
- Launch the Real Eco Feed with NLP-based content curation.

### Phase 4: Community & Advanced Integrations
- Enable peer benchmarking and community impact dashboards.
- Explore IoT integrations (smart plugs, EV chargers) for automated tracking.
- API expansion for enterprise partnerships (carbon offsetting, green product discounts).

## Final Positioning
EcoTrack AI becomes an AI-powered Sustainability Intelligence Platform that:
1. Measures impact (Accurate tracking)
2. Generates actions (AI recommendations)
3. Simulates outcomes (Interactive scenarios)
4. Forecasts emissions (Predictive analytics)
5. Scores sustainability (Gamification)
6. Delivers environmental knowledge (Eco Feed)
7. Tracks progress (Dashboards)
8. Maps SDG impact (Global alignment)
9. **[NEW] Catalyzes Change (Actionable roadmaps with financial and environmental ROI)**
